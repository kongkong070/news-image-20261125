One image summing up the world's news of Wednesday 25 November 2026, as a
newspaper front page: the date in the masthead, six stories across regions
and fields, each with a short headline and one factual line.

- **Shenzhou-24 lifts off** — First Pakistani astronaut reaches the
  Tiangong space station on China's crewed flight.
- **CIA hit by cyberattack** — US spy agencies struck; classified data said
  affected, CNN reported on 25 November.
- **Microsoft Code Red** — Historic outage triggers an emergency response
  at the tech giant.
- **Eisenkot seals deal** — First coalition pact in the bid to form a new
  Israeli government.
- **Record smashed in Doha** — Suryavanshi, 15, hits 204 off 53 balls, a
  List A world record.
- **WHO flees Ebola zone** — Health workers evacuated from a hospital in
  DR Congo amid fierce fighting.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) draws the front page itself —
bitmap font, emblems and layout in one file, PNG encoded by hand — and
writes it to `/output/entry.png`. Two runs produce byte-identical output.
The image is 1024×1024, under 1 MiB.
